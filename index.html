FAQ

Questions

Is So Widget free?

Yes. No ads, no subscriptions, no account.

Which phones are supported?

Android 7.0 or newer. Wallpaper color suggestions use Android 8.1 or newer; on older phones they come from the last wallpaper you set in the app. iOS is not supported yet.

Why does it ask for notification access?

Only to read the song that is playing so the media widgets can show it. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere.

Do widgets drain the battery?

No. Widgets are drawn once and refreshed about every 5 minutes by Android's own job scheduler. Media widgets update only when the music changes.

Who owns the wallpapers?

Every wallpaper belongs to its original artist and is loaded from Wallhaven. So Widget does not host images, credits the uploader and is not affiliated with Wallhaven. To report an image, use Wallhaven's DMCA page.
