Is it really free?+

Yes. All 60 widgets, Studio themes and wallpaper browsing are free. No ads, no subscriptions, no account to create.

Will it work on my phone?+

So Widget supports Android 7.0 and newer. Wallpaper color suggestions use Android 8.1 or newer; older phones use the last wallpaper you set in the app. iOS is not supported yet.

Why does it need notification access?+

Media widgets use notification access to read the currently playing song and provide playback controls. This information is not stored or sent anywhere.

What about my battery?+

Widgets are designed to keep background work light. Most refresh about every five minutes through Android's job scheduler; media widgets respond to playback changes. Actual battery use depends on the widgets and your device.

Where do the wallpapers come from?+

Wallpapers are loaded from Wallhaven and belong to their original artists. So Widget shows SFW content, credits the uploader and source, and keeps credits in downloaded files. Wallpapers are for personal use. So Widget is not affiliated with Wallhaven. Reports can be made through Wallhaven's DMCA page.
